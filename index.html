My Shelves

Your books are saved in this browser on this device. Export a backup to Proton Drive or another cloud now and then, and use Import to move them to another device. On a phone, Export opens the share menu so you can send it straight to the Proton Drive app.

Google Books key (optional)

Searches use Open Library, Google Books and the national libraries of Norway, Spain and France. Google's free shared allowance often runs out, so add your own free key to get its results every time: how to get a key.

Book details from Open Library, Google Books, the National Library of Norway, the National Library of Spain, the National Library of France, the National Library of Portugal (Portuguese National Bibliography, CC0) and the Brazilian ISBN Agency (CBL, via BrasilAPI).
